How the TikTok workflow works

Account access and every draft transfer are initiated by the creator or an authorized account manager.

Connect with Login Kit

The user chooses to connect a TikTok account and authorizes access through TikTok Login Kit.

Review analytics

RoboBella Analytics displays the authorized creator's profile, available statistics and recent public video metrics.

Choose a video

The user selects a local video and authorizes the TikTok account for the draft upload before sending it.

Draft Upload keeps publishing in the creator's hands

The selected video is transferred using TikTok's video.upload permission and delivered to the authorized creator's TikTok Inbox as a draft. RoboBella Analytics does not automatically publish the video. The creator reviews and edits the draft, then chooses whether to publish it inside TikTok.

TikTok Draft Upload

Creator-controlled

Send one locally selected video to the authorized TikTok creator's Inbox as a draft for review and editing.

This does not publish your video. TikTok sends the draft to your Inbox, where you can edit and publish it manually.

After authorization, choose Upload to TikTok as draft in the secure upload panel below.

Supported formats: MP4, QuickTime and WebM. Maximum size: 40 MB.

Draft uploads require the TikTok video.upload permission from the connected creator account.

Draft-upload authorization is required before a video can be selected.

About RoboBella Analytics

For creators and account managers

RoboBella Analytics is for creators and the people they authorize to manage creator accounts. TikTok users connect through Login Kit, review available profile and video performance information, and choose whether to send a selected video to TikTok as a draft.

Data & Privacy

Account access is limited to information the user authorizes. Connected account data is used for the service's analytics and draft workflow. See the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service for details.

Support and data requests

For account support or a data deletion request, contact the service administrator using the contact details provided during account authorization. The Privacy Policy explains how information is handled and how to request deletion.
