RoboBella Project

RoboBella Analytics

A social media analytics and reporting service that helps authorized creators review account growth, content performance and audience engagement across supported platforms.

YouTube

Tracks channel growth, video performance, watch time, retention and series-level results.

Instagram

Tracks profile growth, reach, views, saves, shares and post-level insights for authorized RoboBella accounts.

TikTok

TikTok account analytics integration is currently being prepared.

How it works

Users connect only social media accounts they own or are authorized to manage. RoboBella Analytics retrieves permitted profile, content and performance data, then organizes it into reporting dashboards for performance review.

Data access

Access is limited to data authorized by the account owner. RoboBella Analytics does not sell personal information or publish connected account data publicly.
